# FronteraEval design system

The public interface is an editorial research tool, not a benchmark dashboard.

## Principles

1. The user's decision comes before the catalogue structure.
2. The inference boundary is the primary visual concept.
3. Typography remains tool-sized; hierarchy comes from space, rules and wording.
4. Reviewed, catalogued and imported records must remain visibly distinct.
5. No universal score, card wall, decorative metrics or false comparability.
6. Red is reserved for limits, cautions and active emphasis.

The reference direction is calm, progressive disclosure with an editorial reading rhythm: restrained serif headings, quiet metadata, warm neutral surfaces and minimal ornament.
