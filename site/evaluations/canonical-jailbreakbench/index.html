OriginJailbreakBench project and paper authorsTopicsSafeguards · Evaluation integrityStatusReviewed

Can support

Robustness to the included jailbreak artifacts under the exact target-model and evaluation configuration.

Cannot support by itself

Universal jailbreak resistance, adaptive adversary performance, agentic misuse, operational incident rates, or a complete safety case.

Decision use

Best used for

Reproducible attack and defense comparison and tracking known jailbreak classes.

Not enough for

Certifying that a model or product is safe from jailbreaks in deployment.
